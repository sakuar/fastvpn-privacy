Introduction

Welcome to OpenGate VPN ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect (and what we do not), how we use it, and how we protect it when you use our mobile application ("the App").

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

02

Information We Do NOT Collect

Your privacy is our core commitment. We want to be very clear about what we never do:

  • We do not log your browsing activity or the content of your traffic
  • We do not record your connection timestamps or session duration
  • We do not store your original IP address on our servers
  • We do not monitor or inspect the websites you visit
  • We do not track DNS queries made through our VPN tunnel
  • We do not sell, trade, or rent any user data to third parties
🔒 Strict No-Log Policy — We have zero knowledge of what you do online while connected to our service.
03

Information We Collect

To provide and improve the service, we may collect the following minimal, anonymous information:

  • Aggregate bandwidth usage — total data transferred per day (not linked to any identity), used to manage server capacity
  • Connection success/failure events — anonymous counts to monitor service health
  • App crash reports — technical diagnostics (device model, OS version, stack trace) to fix bugs; no personal identifiers included
  • Advertising identifiers — provided by the operating system (e.g., Android Advertising ID), used by our ad partners to serve relevant ads; you may reset or opt out of this at any time via your device settings

None of the above can be used to identify you personally or trace your online activity.

04

Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

The App is free to use and is supported by advertisements. We integrate third-party advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob) to display ads. These partners may collect:

  • Device advertising identifier
  • General device information (model, OS version, screen size)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address, not GPS)

These ad partners operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them:

  • Google AdMob — Google Privacy Policy

We do not share any VPN traffic data, browsing history, or connection logs with advertising partners.

05

How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Operating, maintaining, and improving the VPN service
  • Monitoring server health and allocating resources
  • Diagnosing and resolving technical issues
  • Displaying advertisements to support the free service

We do not use any collected data for user profiling, behavioral tracking, or selling to third parties.

06

Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the limited data we process. These include encrypted communications, secure server infrastructure, and restricted access controls.

Since we do not collect personal data or activity logs, the risk of data breach impacting your privacy is minimal. Anonymous aggregate statistics are stored securely and automatically purged on a regular cycle.

07

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

08

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access — request to know what data we hold about you (in most cases, none)
  • Deletion — request deletion of any data associated with you
  • Withdraw consent — stop using the App at any time; uninstalling removes all local data from your device

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.
